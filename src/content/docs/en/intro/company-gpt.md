innFactory CompanyGPT is your secure and flexible AI assistant for maximum productivity, based on the intuitive interface and functions of ChatGPT.

Automate your processes, reduce costs, and easily meet legal requirements such as the GDPR and the EU AI Act. With our innFactory CompanyGPT, you retain full control over your data while making processes more efficient with AI and future-proofing your business.

Agent mode allows you to easily create your own AI agents using natural language, but also to individually connect more complex applications via REST API or MCP. We are also happy to expand the functionality of your environment with the n8n AI automation platform.

## Advantages

- Fast implementation
- Transparent pricing
- GDPR and EU AI Act compliant
- Modularity
- Corporate branding
- Compliance & training included
