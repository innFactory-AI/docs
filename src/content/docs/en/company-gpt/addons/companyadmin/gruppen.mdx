Groups bundle people. Above all they decide **with whom something is shared**: who sees agents, prompts, skills and MCP servers depends on groups. Groups can also carry rights in the extensions and settings of their own.

What someone may do in CompanyGPT (features) and which models they see depends on their [role](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/rollen-und-rechte/), not on groups.

You find the groups under **Access** → **Groups**.

## Kinds of groups

| Source | Origin | Members |
|---|---|---|
| **Local** | Created in companyADMIN | You maintain them yourself |
| **Entra** | Groups from Microsoft Entra ID | Come from Entra, at sign-in or through the group sync; read-only in companyADMIN |
| **OIDC** | Groups from the sign-in's group claim (OIDC) | Come at sign-in; read-only in companyADMIN |

CompanyGPT only knows an Entra group once a member has signed in or you have registered it in companyADMIN.

## Group list

By default the list shows only groups that are used somewhere. **Show all groups** adds the others. A search always finds every group.

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Name of the group; badge **Duplicate** if the same Entra group was created twice |
| **Source** | **Local**, **Entra** or **OIDC** |
| **Members** | Number of members |
| **Linked to** | For local groups: linked Entra groups |
| **Last synced** | Last reconciliation of the members |
| **Used in** | Rights, settings and shares attached to the group |

With **Search name or Entra ID** you also find a group by its object ID.

## Creating a group

Administrators with the right **Manage groups** see the **Create group** section on the group list.

1. Enter a **Name** and optionally a **Description**. The name is always required, for Entra groups too.
2. Choose the **Source**: **Local** or **Entra**. For **Entra**, also enter the group's **Entra object ID**. Upper or lower case does not matter.
3. Click **Create**.

With the Entra group sync set up, companyADMIN checks the object ID in Entra ID, replaces the name you entered with the name from Entra and adds the members right away. For large groups the members are loaded in the background. Without the group sync the group is only registered; its members then arrive at sign-in.

An Entra group that already exists cannot be created a second time. companyADMIN then offers **Open the existing group**.

## Syncing Entra groups

Without a sync, CompanyGPT knows the memberships of an Entra group only from its members' sign-ins. The group sync reads the members of all Entra groups CompanyGPT knows from Microsoft Graph and enters them. It never creates new groups.

innFactory sets up the group sync for your environment. In addition it needs admin consent in Microsoft Entra ID for the permission `GroupMember.Read.All`.

With the right **Manage groups** you find the action **Sync Entra groups** at the top of the group list. It opens the sync area, which shows:

- **Last run** with its result (**successful**, **partly** or **failed**)
- **Daily**: whether the sync runs automatically every day, and when the next run is
- **Owners count as members**: whether owners of an Entra group count as members

To sync by hand:

1. At the top of the group list, click **Sync Entra groups**.
2. Click **Preview**. The preview says how many groups were read and which people join (**joins**) or drop out (**drops out**).
3. Click **Sync now**.

companyADMIN and the extensions see the result at once, CompanyGPT within five minutes. Groups that Microsoft Graph does not allow to be read, or for which Graph returns no members, stay unchanged and are reported as not applied.

If companyADMIN reports that Microsoft Graph refuses the app, the admin consent for `GroupMember.Read.All` is usually missing. If the sync is not set up, the action cannot be clicked and companyADMIN gives the reason **The Entra group sync is not set up.** In that case contact innFactory.

## A group's detail page

The detail page has the tabs **Overview**, **Content**, **Extensions**, **Configuration** and **Members**, plus the danger zone **Delete group**.

### Overview

Name, description, **Entra object ID**, **Source**, **Members**, **Last synced** and **Used in**. You change name and description only for local groups, with the right **Manage groups**; apply the change with **Save**. For Entra and OIDC groups nothing can be changed on the overview.

If the same Entra group exists twice, **Created twice** appears. Merging is manual: move rights and shares to one group, then delete the other.

### Content

What is shared with the group: agents, prompts, skills and MCP servers, each with the right **View** or **Edit**. You change this on the content itself, see [Content and visibility](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/inhalte/).

:::note[Without the superadmin features]
Without the superadmin features the tab only shows content within your own scope: library-managed, public and shared with you. Everything else appears only as a count ("N more items not visible (you lack the rights for them)."), and MCP servers outside your scope only with their name.
:::

### Extensions

The rights all members of the group receive in the extensions, for example **Use knowledge** or **Use courses**. A tick grants the right. All rights are described in [Extensions](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/erweiterungen/).

:::caution
Every member gets a right granted to a group, and whoever manages the group in Entra ID thereby manages the right. Grant managing rights only to security groups with restricted owners, not to Microsoft 365 groups that people can join themselves.
:::

In some environments granting to groups is locked, for example as long as CompanyGPT does not know any members of the Entra groups yet. The tab then shows **Group rights locked** with the reason.

### Configuration

Settings that apply to the group's members: memories, retention, agent builder and file uploads. Without a value of its own, the role or the base configuration applies. A value on the role always wins. Avoid different values in several groups of the same person; set such values on the role instead.

Models are released per role, not per group. **Go to roles** takes you there.

### Members

- **Local groups**: in the **Add person** section you search for people by name or email and add them. People you remove lose everything released to this group.
- **Entra and OIDC groups**: the members come from Entra ID or from the sign-in and can only be viewed here.

In the **Link with Entra group** section you can couple a local group to one or more Entra groups. Pick the Entra groups and store the link with **Save**. Only with a saved link does **Reconcile now** copy their members: new ones are added, and anyone no longer in any of the Entra groups is removed, including people added by hand. Only people who have signed in at least once are included.

### Deleting a group

**Delete group** removes the group with every right, share and setting attached to it. This cannot be undone. An Entra group stays in Entra ID; when a member signs in, CompanyGPT creates it again without rights.
