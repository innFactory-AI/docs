Addons extend CompanyGPT with additional applications: making documents searchable, generating files, translating, transcribing, learning and administering CompanyGPT. Many addons have their own interface and also provide their functions as tools for agents in the chat (via [MCP servers](/en/company-gpt/integrationen/mcp-server/)).

## Where to find the addons

You reach the addons with their own interface that are enabled for you through **[All apps](/en/company-gpt/addons/alle-apps/)**: as a page of its own and in the chat through the **Apps** area in the sidebar. companyM365 and companyKNOWLEDGE have no tile – you use them in the chat or in Office.

The interfaces of companyRAG, companyFILES, companyTRANSLATE, companyTRANSCRIBE, companyLEARN, companyADMIN, the companyM365 admin area and All apps share the same structure. They have the same sidebar with **New chat** and **All apps**, the same account menu with theme and language, and the same sign-in as the chat. For details, see [Shared interface](/en/company-gpt/addons/gemeinsame-oberflaeche/).

## Who may use which addons

Which addons are available to you is decided by your organization. Depending on the environment, administrators grant the permissions for them in [companyADMIN](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/erweiterungen/) or through groups in your directory that innFactory maintains. If an addon you need is missing, contact your administrator.
