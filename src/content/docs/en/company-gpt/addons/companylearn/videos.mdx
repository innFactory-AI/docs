---
title: Videos
description: Upload videos for companyLEARN (MP4 and WebM), use them in lessons, play them back and delete them from the video library.
---

Video lessons draw on companyLEARN's **Video library**. You upload a video once and can then use it in as many lessons as you like, including lessons in different courses. The video library is available to people with the **Manage courses** permission; you open it via **Videos** in the menu.

## Supported formats

| Format | File extension |
|---|---|
| MP4 | `.mp4` |
| WebM | `.webm` |

On upload, companyLEARN checks the file extension, the declared file type and the file's content; other formats are rejected. By default, a file can be up to 2 GB in size.

companyLEARN stores videos unchanged and does not convert them. Learners therefore see exactly the file you upload, so it has to play in common browsers.

## Uploading a video

You have two options:

- **In the video library:** Click **Upload video** at the top right and choose the file. A progress bar shows the upload; afterwards the video appears in the table.
- **Directly in a lesson:** In the dialog of a video lesson, click **Choose a video…** and then **Upload a new video** in the **Choose a video** dialog. After the upload, the new video is selected for the lesson.

If the upload fails, the message "The upload failed. Please try again." appears.

## The video library

The **Video library** table shows all uploaded videos:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **Filename** | Name of the uploaded file |
| **Type** | `video/mp4` or `video/webm` |
| **Size** | File size |
| **Used in** | Number of lessons that use the video |
| **Created** | Upload date |
| **Actions** | **Delete** (trash can) |

## Using a video in a lesson

In the dialog of a video lesson, **Choose a video…** opens the list of all videos with filename and size. Click a video to select it; the selected video is marked with a check mark. Then save the lesson. How to create lessons is described under [Managing courses](/en/company-gpt/addons/companylearn/kurse-verwalten/#lessons).

## Playback

Learners watch the video in their browser's player with the usual controls; skipping forward and back is possible. When a video plays to the end, the lesson counts as done. In the chat, the video plays right in the conversation (see [In the chat](/en/company-gpt/addons/companylearn/im-chat/)).

A video can only be played by people who are enrolled in a published course with that lesson and have unlocked the corresponding chapter. The playback addresses are only valid for a short time; companyLEARN renews them automatically during playback.

The video library itself has no preview. To see a video the way learners do, open the lesson in a published course you are enrolled in.

If a video cannot be loaded, the message "The video could not be loaded. Please try again later." appears.

## Deleting a video

Click the trash can in the video's row and confirm **Delete video?**. The file is deleted permanently.

A video that is still used in lessons cannot be deleted. companyLEARN then reports, for example, "This video is used by 2 lessons. Remove it from those lessons first." Choose a different video in those lessons or delete the lessons, then delete the video again.
