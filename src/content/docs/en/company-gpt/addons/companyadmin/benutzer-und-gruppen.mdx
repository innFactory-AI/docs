Under **Access** you manage who uses CompanyGPT and in which context. The **Users**, **Groups** and **Admin sources** tabs complement the [roles](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/rollen-und-rechte/).

The guiding principle of the page: roles bundle permissions and capabilities, groups bundle users – both cascade to their members.

## Users

The **Users** tab lists all users of the environment. You open a user and assign them a role. Since every user has exactly one role, the assignment replaces the previous role.

## Groups

Groups are the basis for sharing agents, prompts and skills. Two kinds are available:

- **Local groups** that you create and maintain in CompanyGPT itself.
- **Entra groups** that you register so that membership is taken from Microsoft Entra ID.

When setting the [visibility of content](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/inhalte/) you then pick exactly these groups.

## Admin sources

The **Admin sources** tab shows who has administration access and where that access comes from – for example from a role or a [direct grant](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/direkte-berechtigungen/). It is the quickest answer to the question of why a person can see the administration area.

:::note
For permanent responsibilities, assign a role. Direct grants are meant for individual exceptions.
:::
