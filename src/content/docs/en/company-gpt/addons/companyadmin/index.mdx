companyADMIN is the administration area of CompanyGPT. Here you see how CompanyGPT is used and what it costs, check the operational status, decide who may do what, control the visibility of agents, prompts, skills and MCP tools, and configure models, agents and extensions.

## Opening companyADMIN

You open companyADMIN from **All apps** in the CompanyGPT sidebar. There are two tiles:

- **Admin** opens the administration. Only administrators see this tile.
- **Insights** opens the usage and cost overview. Everyone who may view Insights sees this tile.

You can also reach companyADMIN directly at `https://COMPANY.company-gpt.com/companygpt/admin/`. The start page is **Insights** if you may view Insights, otherwise **Status**.

## The interface

companyADMIN uses the same interface as the other extensions: a sidebar with **New chat** and **All apps**, the menu below, and the account menu at the bottom. How this interface works is described in [Shared interface](/en/company-gpt/addons/gemeinsame-oberflaeche/).

The companyADMIN menu has five sections. The sub-pages of the section that is open are indented below it.

| Section | Sub-pages | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Insights** | – | Usage, costs and activity of the environment |
| **Status** | – | What is in force right now and whether everything is running, plus the logs |
| **Access** | **Users**, **Roles**, **Groups**, **Direct grants** | Who may do what and who belongs to which group |
| **Content** | **Agents**, **Prompts**, **Skills**, **MCP tools**, **Categories**, **Library Sync** | Visibility and origin of the content in CompanyGPT |
| **Configuration** | **Providers and models**, **Model access**, **Labels**, **Agent capabilities**, **Agent runtime**, **MCP domains**, **Baseline & adjustments**, **Tools**, **Extensions** | How CompanyGPT and the extensions are set up |

Clicking a row in a list usually opens a detail page with tabs. Deleting lives there in the **Danger zone** and always asks first.

In the account menu you set **Theme** and **Language** (Deutsch or English). It also shows your email address and, below it, whether you are a **Superadmin** or an **Admin (no superadmin)**.

## Administration and Insights

companyADMIN has two kinds of access:

- **Administration** opens every section. Who is an administrator follows from the admin sources of your environment. Depending on the environment these include the ADMIN role, an admin group from Microsoft Entra ID, fixed people, or the right **Access the admin area** on a role. Which sources apply is shown under [Status → Admin sources](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/status/).
- **Insights** opens the Insights section only. The right **View Insights** is enough, which you grant to roles or groups. Administrators hold it automatically. Details are in [Insights](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/insights/).

## Superadmin and admin

The account menu shows which view you have. The difference mainly concerns other people's private content:

| | Superadmin | Admin (no superadmin) |
|---|---|---|
| **Access → Users** | List of all people, change the role directly on the person | Not visible. You assign roles under the role → **Members** |
| Lists under **Content** | Every agent, prompt and skill, including private ones | Public, shared-with-you and library-managed items |
| Visibility of self-created content | Can be changed | Display only |
| Visibility of synced content | Can be changed | Can be changed |
| MCP servers created by users | Sharing can be changed, details visible even for private servers | Sharing display only, details of private servers hidden |
| **Insights → Agents** | Every agent individually | Other people's private agents only as a sum |

Whether the superadmin features are switched on for your organization's administrators is something you agree with innFactory. innFactory switches them on at your request.

## Roles, groups and visibility

Three concepts carry the whole area:

- **Role = what someone may do.** A role sets features (such as creating agents or web search), models, agent capabilities and rights in the extensions. Everyone has exactly **one** role. Rights from several roles are not added up; if a group of people needs the rights of two roles, create a role of its own for them.
- **Group = with whom something is shared.** Groups decide who sees agents, prompts, skills and MCP servers. Groups can also carry rights in the extensions and settings of their own. A person can be in many groups.
- **Visibility** decides per item who sees it: only the owner, all users of the environment, or specific groups.

## Areas at a glance

**Insights and status**

- [Insights](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/insights/) – usage, tokens, estimated costs and activity
- [Status](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/status/) – reachability, active configuration, roles and admin sources
- [Logs](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/audit-log/) – who changed what in companyADMIN and in CompanyGPT

**Access**

- [Users](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/nutzer/) – people, their role and their effective rights
- [Roles](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/rollen-und-rechte/) – create roles, set rights, assign members
- [Groups](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/gruppen/) – local and Entra groups, group sync
- [Direct grants](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/direkte-berechtigungen/) – every right and who holds it

**Content**

- [Content and visibility](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/inhalte/) – agents, prompts, skills and MCP tools
- [Categories](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/kategorien/) – categories of the agent marketplace
- [Library Sync](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/library-sync/) – sync content from Git repositories

**Configuration**

- [Providers and models](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/modelle/) – model access per role and labels
- [Agent capabilities](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/agent-capabilities/) – what the agent builder offers
- [Agent runtime](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/agent-laufzeit/) – scheduled chats, tool approval, subagents and settings per role or group
- [MCP domains](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/mcp-domains/) – allowed domains for MCP servers
- [Baseline & adjustments](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/vorgabe-und-anpassungen/) – what the deployment ships and what was adjusted
- [Tools](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/tools/) – custom tiles under All apps
- [Extensions](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/erweiterungen/) – rights for companyFILES, companyRAG, companyTRANSLATE and all other extensions
