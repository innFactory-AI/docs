---
title: companyTRANSLATE
description: Translation app in CompanyGPT for text and whole documents, with glossaries for consistent terminology and translation in the chat.
---

companyTRANSLATE translates text and whole documents. Glossaries let you define how technical terms, product names or fixed phrases are translated. Agents in the CompanyGPT chat can use companyTRANSLATE as well and translate files or text directly in the chat.

## Opening and signing in

You open companyTRANSLATE in CompanyGPT via [**All apps**](/en/company-gpt/addons/alle-apps/) and the **Translate** tile.

companyTRANSLATE uses your CompanyGPT session. If you are signed in to CompanyGPT, you are signed in to companyTRANSLATE too; there are no separate credentials. This applies regardless of the sign-in method your organization uses for CompanyGPT.

## Interface

companyTRANSLATE uses the [shared interface of the CompanyGPT extensions](/en/company-gpt/addons/gemeinsame-oberflaeche/). The sidebar holds **New chat** and **All apps**, followed by the three areas of the application. The account menu offers **Theme** and **Language**, among other things; the interface is available in German and English.

| Area | Page | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | [Translate text](/en/company-gpt/addons/company-translate/text-uebersetzen/) | Translate text in real time, up to 5,000 characters |
| **Documents** | [Translate document](/en/company-gpt/addons/company-translate/dokument-uebersetzen/) | Translate whole files up to 40 MB with formatting preserved |
| **Glossaries** | [Glossaries](/en/company-gpt/addons/company-translate/glossare/) | Manage personal and global glossaries |

An area's actions sit on the right of the page header: **New translation** in **Text** and **Documents**, **Upload glossary** and – for administrators – **Upload global glossary** in **Glossaries**. On narrow screens you open the areas via the menu in the header bar; the **Glossaries** area is not available on smartphones.

If you drag a file onto the **Text** page, companyTRANSLATE switches to the **Documents** area and picks up the file there.

## Access and permissions

Your administrator decides who may use companyTRANSLATE – in companyADMIN via the [extension permissions](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/erweiterungen/) or, depending on the environment, via groups. companyADMIN has two permissions for this:

- **Use translation** – translate text and documents, manage your own glossaries
- **Manage glossaries** – additionally create, upload and delete global glossaries for everyone

If you lack the usage permission, companyTRANSLATE shows the **No access** page. The same permissions apply when an agent translates for you in the chat.

## Translation services and data flow

companyTRANSLATE does not translate by itself; it hands the content to a translation service. The default service is Microsoft's **Azure AI Translator**. **Google Cloud Translation** is optional; whether it is enabled in your environment is decided by your organization.

| Content | Translation service |
|---|---|
| Documents in the **Documents** area | Azure AI Translator |
| Text in the **Text** area | Azure AI Translator; if Google is enabled in your environment, text translation runs via Google Cloud Translation |
| Glossaries | are sent to the service along with the translation; if Google is enabled, uploaded glossaries are also stored with Google so that they apply in text translation |
| [Translating in the chat](/en/company-gpt/addons/company-translate/im-chat/) | Azure AI Translator; Google only for text with a glossary or on explicit request, and only if it is enabled |

For translation, the content – text, files and glossary terms – is transmitted to the respective service. companyTRANSLATE stores uploaded documents and their translations in the storage of your CompanyGPT environment and deletes them after 30 days (see [Translation history](#translation-history-and-retention)).

## Supported formats and limits

| Area | Input | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | Direct entry in the interface | 5,000 characters per translation |
| **Documents** | PDF, Word (`.docx`), PowerPoint (`.pptx`), Excel (`.xlsx`), TXT, Markdown (`.md`), HTML | 40 MB per file |
| **Glossaries** | CSV, TSV (`.tsv`/`.tab`), XLIFF (`.xlf`), TMX | The translation services limit glossaries to 10 MB; larger TMX files are reduced to a term glossary |
| **In the chat** | Files in the **Documents** formats, plus text | Files up to 40 MB, text up to 50,000 characters (up to 30,000 characters via Google) |

## Translation history and retention

In the **Documents** area, **Recent translations** lists your document translations – including those an agent created for you in the chat. Each entry shows the file name, target language, file size and time. Running translations are marked **Processing**, failed ones **Failed**; **Retry translation** in the entry's menu starts them again.

Translations are kept for 30 days. After that they disappear from the history and can no longer be downloaded or retried; companyTRANSLATE then reports "This translation has expired and is no longer available". Download results you need for longer in good time.
