**Access** → **Users** lists everyone who has signed in to CompanyGPT at least once. People who have never signed in do not appear here.

:::note[Superadmins only]
Only superadmins see the user list. Without the superadmin features the menu entry is missing, and opening the page directly leads to **Roles**. You then assign roles through the role on its **Members** tab, see [Roles](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/rollen-und-rechte/). The account menu shows whether you are a superadmin.
:::

## The list

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Display name |
| **Email** | Email address |
| **Role** | The person's one role |
| **Last sign-in** | Approximate time of the last sign-in |
| **Origin of the role** | **System**, **Role sync** (automatically from Entra ID), **Manual**, or the admin group through which someone becomes ADMIN |

Use **Search name or email** and the **Role** filter to narrow the list. Clicking a row opens the detail page.

## Detail page

### Overview

The **Person** section shows email, name, **Entra object ID**, **Signs in via**, **Account created** and **Last sign-in (approximate)**. CompanyGPT stores no sign-in time; what is shown is the last change to the account, which happens at every sign-in.

In the **Role** section you change the person's role with the **Role** picker. The change is saved immediately and applies immediately; if CompanyGPT is open, the person may need to reload the page. Since everyone has exactly one role, the new role replaces the previous one.

:::caution
If automatic role assignment from Entra ID (role sync) is active in your environment, CompanyGPT sets the role again from the Entra groups at the next sign-in. A role set here is lost then. The page points this out.
:::

If the person's groups or roles have changed since their last sign-in, **Takes effect at the next sign-in** appears with the new groups or roles. Where the displayed group memberships come from (sign-in or group sync) is shown below.

### Effective rights

**Effective rights** lists every right of the person with its source:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **Right** | The right, for example **Access the admin area** or **Use translation** |
| **Kind** | **System**, **Extension** or **companyADMIN** |
| **Source** | Where the right comes from: **Role …**, **Group …**, **Everyone**, **Default for admins** or **Setting …** |

Below, **companyGPT features** shows what the person may do in CompanyGPT (**Allowed** or **Not allowed**). These feature rights are set by the role; you change them there on the **companyGPT** tab.

### Models

Which models the person sees, per provider. Models blocked by their role are listed under **Hidden:**.

### Groups

All of the person's groups with their **Source** (**Local**, **Entra** or **OIDC**) and member count.

### Activity

**First use**, **Last use**, **Messages in total** and **Conversations in total**. **Open in Insights** opens the usage in [Insights](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/insights/).
