Under **Content** you see which agents, prompts, skills and MCP servers exist in CompanyGPT, where they come from and who sees them. The sub-pages are **Agents**, **Prompts**, **Skills**, **MCP tools**, **Categories** and **Library Sync**. This page describes the first four; [Categories](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/kategorien/) and [Library Sync](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/library-sync/) have pages of their own.

## What you see

What appears in the lists depends on whether you are a superadmin:

- **Superadmins** see every agent, prompt and skill, including other people's private content.
- **Admins without the superadmin features** see public, shared-with-them and library-managed items. A notice above the list explains this restricted view.

The account menu shows whether you are a superadmin.

## Agents, prompts and skills

### Lists

| List | Columns | Search |
|---|---|---|
| **Agents** | Name, **Owner**, **Model**, **Visibility**, **Origin**, **Requests (30d)**, **Last used** | **Search name, description or repo** |
| **Prompts** | Name, **Description**, **Visibility**, **Origin** | **Search name, command or repo** |
| **Skills** | Name, **Description**, **Visibility**, **Origin** | **Search name, description or repo** |

Promoted agents carry the badge **Promoted**.

The **Visibility** column shows **Owner only**, **Public** or **Groups (n)**. The **Origin** column shows:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **manual** | Created in CompanyGPT |
| **synced** | Comes from a Git repository through [Library Sync](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/library-sync/) |
| **modified in LibreChat** | Synced, but changed in CompanyGPT since. What the next sync does with it is described in [Library Sync](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/library-sync/) |

Filters:

- **Origin**: **From the library** or **Created in companyGPT**
- **Visible to**
- **Changes**: **Changed in companyGPT** or **Same as the repository**

The lists load more entries as you scroll. At most 1,000 entries are shown; search and filters then work on those only.

### Detail pages

Clicking a row opens the detail page. You change an item's configuration in CompanyGPT; in companyADMIN you review it and control its visibility.

**Agent**

- **Overview**: owner, model, category, usage over the last 30 days and the chart **Requests per day (30d)**
- **Configuration**: **Instructions**, **Tools** with the **Loading** column, **MCP servers**, **Skills**, **Subagents & handoff edges** and **Code & memory**
- **Visibility**
- **Origin**

**Prompt**

- **Overview**: **Text (production version)**, **Settings** such as **Command** and category, and the **Versions**. The sync replaces only the production version; older versions are kept.
- **Visibility**
- **Origin**

**Skill**

- **Overview**: **Behaviour** with **Invoked by users**, **Invoked by the model**, **Always applied**, **Allowed tools** and **Version**
- **Content**: **Instructions (SKILL.md)**, **Files** and **Frontmatter**
- **Visibility**
- **Origin**

### Origin

For synced content the **Origin** tab shows **Repository** (with a link to Library Sync), **Path**, **Commit**, **Last written** and **State**. If it says the item was changed in CompanyGPT since the last sync, the next sync overwrites that change. Only for a repository with **We own this repo (allow write-back/up-sync)** do changed agents and skills stay until they are written back, as long as their file is in the repository.

For content created in CompanyGPT it says **Created in companyGPT, not from the library.**

## Setting visibility

You set the visibility on the detail page on the **Visibility** tab. It names the owner (**Owned by …**) and offers three levels:

- **Owner only** – no share with everyone and no group shares. Shares with individual people or roles stay.
- **Public — all users of this environment** – everyone in your environment sees the item, with read access.
- **Specific groups only** – the selected groups and the owner see the item. With **Search groups…** you find groups by name or Entra object ID. You cannot save without selecting a group.

Confirm with **Save**.

:::caution[Saving replaces existing shares]
Saving always stores the chosen level as a whole. It replaces the previous share with everyone and the previous group shares.

- Switching to **Public** or **Owner only** removes every group share, including editor shares that people made in CompanyGPT. **Public** gives everyone read access only.
- For synced content the owner is the system account. **Owner only** there means: no user sees the item.
:::

An Entra group can only be selected once CompanyGPT knows it, that is once a member has signed in or the group was created or synced. Shares made in companyADMIN give read access (**Viewer**). The levels **Editor** and **Owner** come from shares that people made in CompanyGPT themselves.

### Who may change visibility

| Content | May be changed by |
|---|---|
| Synced (from the library) | Any administrator with the matching manage right (**Manage agents**, **Manage prompts** or **Manage skills**) |
| Created in CompanyGPT (**manual**) | Superadmins only. Other administrators see the visibility and a notice |

The owner of an item can still change its sharing in CompanyGPT, as far as their role allows sharing. Whether a role may share or share publicly at all is decided by the feature rights of the [role](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/rollen-und-rechte/).

For content created in CompanyGPT, **Also individually shared with:** lists shares with individual people or roles. Superadmins take them back with **Revoke**.

### Synced content and the repo default

Synced content gets its visibility from the repository (**Managed by the repo default (Library Sync)**). If you change the visibility here, **Individually managed (overrides the repo default)** applies: Library Sync then leaves this item's visibility alone. **Reset to repo default** hands it back to the repository; this applies from the next sync.

### Visibility for several items

In the **Agents**, **Prompts** and **Skills** lists you set the visibility for several items at once. The bar for this only appears with the matching manage right (**Manage agents**, **Manage prompts** or **Manage skills**).

1. Select the rows with the checkboxes.
2. Choose the visibility in the bar above the table.
3. Click **Apply to selection**. **Public** asks first.

Only synced items are changed. All others are skipped; you set their visibility on their detail pages.

Every changed item is then **Individually managed (overrides the repo default)**. Later changes to the repository's default visibility no longer reach these items. You hand them back to the repo default only one at a time, on the detail page with **Reset to repo default**.

### Share an agent with yourself

:::note[Superadmins only]
Superadmins see other people's private agents in the list, but cannot simply use them in CompanyGPT. **Share with me** adds you as an **Owner** of the agent.
:::

- On an agent's detail page, **Share with me** is at the top right.
- In the **Agents** list, select several rows and click **Share N agents with me** in the bar above the table.

The agent then appears in your agent list in CompanyGPT. As an owner you can share it further there with the share dialog. An existing share with everyone stays as it is, and the previous owners keep their rights.

The button is hidden when you can already share the agent through a share of your own, for example because you own it. Every share is recorded in the [audit log](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/audit-log/).

## MCP tools

**MCP tools** lists every MCP server of your environment. companyADMIN never shows credentials such as tokens or passwords.

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **From the deployment** | Set up by innFactory in the base configuration; applies as set |
| **From the admin configuration** | Set up through the configuration; applies to the scope shown (everyone, a role or a group) |
| **From a plugin** | Provided by a plugin |
| **Created by a user** | Created by a person in CompanyGPT |
| **agent-only** | Usable only through agents, not directly in the chat |

The **Owner** and **Who sees it** columns show who owns a server and whom it applies to. For servers from the deployment, configuration or a plugin it says **as configured (not per group)**. Use **Search servers** and the **Origin** filter to narrow the list.

An expanded row shows the **Connection**, the address or **Command**, whether **Sign-in via OAuth** is used, and which **User-provided values** the server expects.

- Only servers **Created by a user** have sharing of their own. Only superadmins may change it; the owner can change it in CompanyGPT. Only superadmins see the details of private servers.
- The MCP servers of the extensions (such as companyFILES or companyRAG) are, as a rule, connected for everyone; their tools may therefore also be shown to people without the right to use them, see [Extensions](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/erweiterungen/).
- Which domains MCP servers may use is set under [MCP domains](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/mcp-domains/).
