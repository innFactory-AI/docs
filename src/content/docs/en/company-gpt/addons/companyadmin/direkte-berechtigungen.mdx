---
title: Direct grants
description: Grant individual capabilities such as access to the administration area selectively – as an exception to regular roles.
---

Direct grants assign a single capability selectively, without creating a dedicated role for it. They are meant for one-off exceptions; for permanent responsibilities, [roles](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/rollen-und-rechte/) are the right way.

!["Direct grants" tab with the button for granting a capability](./direct-grants.png)

Use **Grant** to assign a capability. The dialog asks for two values:

- **Role name** – the role the capability applies to
- **Capability** – the capability to grant, for example `access:admin` (access the admin area) or `manage:roles` (manage roles)

![Dialog for granting a direct capability](./grant-dialog.png)

:::note
Direct grants are currently supported for roles only, not for individual people. To give an individual a capability, assign them a suitable role under **Access**.
:::

Every granted and revoked capability is recorded in the [audit log](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/audit-log/). Who currently holds administration access is shown by the **Admin sources** tab under [Users and groups](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/benutzer-und-gruppen/).
