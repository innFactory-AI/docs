title: Quick-Ask and dictation
description: With Quick-Ask you ask companyGPT a quick question from anywhere, including screenshots, and continue chats or Cowork tasks; dictation turns speech into text.
---

**Quick-Ask** is a small window for quick questions that you open from anywhere, without switching to the app. The question goes either to the **Chat** or to **Cowork**, as a new conversation or task, or into one of the last five. You can include screenshots right away.

## Open Quick-Ask

- **macOS:** ⌥Space
- **Windows:** Ctrl+Alt+Space
- or through the app's icon in the menu bar or in the notification area of the taskbar

You change the shortcut in the [settings](/en/company-gpt/desktop-app/einstellungen/). Quick-Ask opens on the screen where your mouse is, centered just above the Dock or taskbar. If screenshots or the history list need more space, the window grows upward.

## To Cowork or to the chat

**Tab** switches between **Cowork** and **Chat**.

- **Cowork:** the question starts a new task in its own folder. With **New task ⌄**, you continue one of the last five tasks instead. If that task is currently running, your message goes in as an addition, just like Enter in the input field.
- **Chat:** the question starts a new chat. With **New chat ⌄**, you continue one of the last five chats. The question is sent immediately.

After sending, the app opens with the task or chat.

## Screenshots

- **Region/window:** on macOS, you drag out a region; press Space to pick a window instead. On Windows, the Snipping Tool opens.
- **Whole screen:** captures the screen Quick-Ask is open on.

You can attach several screenshots per message. They appear as thumbnails, and ✕ removes one. With a screenshot, text is optional. In Cowork, the screenshots are saved as files in the task's folder; in the chat, they are uploaded and sent along as if you had pasted them.

:::note[macOS: Screen Recording permission]
The first time you take a screenshot, macOS asks for the **Screen Recording** permission for companyGPT. After allowing it, restart the app once.
:::

## Keyboard shortcuts in Quick-Ask

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| Enter | Send |
| ⇧Enter | New line |
| Tab | Switch between Cowork and Chat |
| ↓ or ⌘K / Ctrl+K | Open recent chats or tasks |
| Esc | Close the list or Quick-Ask (discards the screenshots) |

If you click into another window, Quick-Ask disappears and keeps the text and screenshots for the next time you open it.

## Dictation

The **microphone** in Quick-Ask and in Cowork's input field records your speech and turns it into text. Speech recognition runs through your company's AI Gateway with your sign-in. The recognized text appears at the cursor position, so you can still edit it before sending.

- The first time, the operating system asks for permission to use the **microphone**.
- If recognition fails, the recording is kept, and you can **try again**.
- You set the speech recognition model in the [settings](/en/company-gpt/desktop-app/einstellungen/).

:::note
Dictation in other apps and live recognition while you speak aren't available yet.
:::
