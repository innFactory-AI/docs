**Insights** shows how your CompanyGPT environment is used: who is active, which agents, models and MCP tools are in demand, how many tokens are consumed and roughly what that costs. Insights replaces the former companyDASHBOARD.

The figures come from pre-computed daily values and are refreshed every 10 minutes. The running day is included live and is not final yet.

## Access

You open Insights from the **Insights** tile under **All apps** or from the companyADMIN menu.

Anyone who holds the right **View Insights** (`ext:admin:insights`) may view Insights:

- The ADMIN role holds the right automatically. Anyone who is an administrator by another path, for example as a fixed person or through the right **Access the admin area** on a custom role, also needs it on their role or group.
- You give it to other people through a role or a group, in each case on the **Extensions** tab (see [Roles](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/rollen-und-rechte/) and [Groups](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/gruppen/)).
- The right cannot be opened to everyone (**For everyone**), because Insights shows every user's costs, tokens and email addresses.

Someone who holds only this right and is not an administrator sees nothing in companyADMIN but **Insights**. Clicking an agent then shows only its usage, not its configuration.

Older ways into Insights still apply. Which ways are set up in your environment is shown under [Status → Admin sources](/en/company-gpt/addons/companyadmin/status/) with the panel role **Insights**.

## Period and export

At the top of the page you choose the **Period**:

- **Current month**
- **Last month**
- **7 days**
- **30 days**
- **Custom** with **From** and **To**, at most 366 days

Each figure compares the chosen period with a **Previous period**:

- For **Current month**, **Last month** and a custom period covering exactly one calendar month, the previous period is the whole previous month. In the running month Insights therefore compares a month that has just started with a full one.
- For **7 days**, **30 days** and every other period it is the equally long period before.

**Export (XLSX)** downloads the figures for the chosen period as an Excel file. It contains the sheets **Overview**, **Models**, **Agents**, **MCP Tools** and **Costs by user**, each with all rows.

## Tabs

### Overview

The overview shows tiles with a trend and a comparison to the previous period:

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active users** | Distinct users who sent at least one message in the period |
| **New users** | Users whose first message ever falls into the period |
| **Churned users** | Users whose last message turned 28 days old within the period |
| **Conversations** | Distinct conversations with at least one message in the period |
| **Requests** | User messages sent to a model or agent |
| **Input tokens** / **Output tokens** | Tokens sent to the models and generated by them |
| **Estimated token cost** | Estimate from fixed token prices, see below |
| **MCP calls** | Calls of MCP tools by agents and models |
| **Files** | Files uploaded in the period |
| **STT**, **TTS**, **OCR**, **Images** | Transcribed audio in seconds, synthesized characters, processed pages and generated images, each with its estimated cost |
| **All users** / **All agents** | All accounts or agents of the environment, independent of the period |

Below follow **Top users** (the 10 users with the most requests in the period) and **Churned users**.

#### Reselling costs

The first tile of the overview, **Reselling costs**, shows the amount the innFactory Hub bills for a calendar month. It is only available for whole months; for other periods it offers **Current month** and **Last month** as quick buttons.

- The running month is marked **provisional** and shows a **Forecast**.
- **As of** gives the date of the billing data. Cloud providers deliver usage with a delay of at least 2–3 days; figures are final up to 15 days after month end.
- Hovering over the amount shows the **Breakdown by workload**.
- The icon at the top right opens the innFactory Hub.

If no billing through the innFactory Hub is set up for your environment, the tile shows **Not configured**.

### Agents

Usage per agent. Clicking a name opens the agent's detail page.

Without the superadmin features you see other people's private agents only as a sum in the row **private agents of other users**, without names or trend.

### Models

Usage per model. Conversations with an agent are attributed to the agent's model.

### MCP tools

Calls per MCP tool and server.

### Costs

- **Cost by user** and **Cost by domain**, each with a search
- **Tokens by provider and model** as a donut chart: the provider on the inner ring, the model on the outer ring

### Activity

**Activity by time of day** is a heat map showing on which days and at which times messages are sent.

## What the costs mean

**Estimated token cost** is an estimate, not an invoice:

- It is calculated from fixed token prices; US dollar prices are taken as euros.
- Discounts and cache tokens are not included.
- Unknown models count as €0.

Binding figures come from the innFactory Hub, in Insights through the **Reselling costs** tile.

## Notes on the figures

- If the last calculation is too long ago, the notice **Insights is not up to date** appears. companyADMIN keeps retrying on its own; if the notice persists, contact innFactory.
- companyADMIN removes the names of deleted accounts from Insights daily.
