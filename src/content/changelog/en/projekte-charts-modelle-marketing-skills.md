Several important updates this week: Projects for better chat organization, additional AI models, automatic company colors in charts, and a comprehensive skill portfolio for marketing and sales.

## ✨ CompanyGPT

- **Projects**: Chats can now be organized by topic into Projects. Projects appear in the sidebar and can be expanded or collapsed. **All Projects** gives you an overview with chat counts, dates, and a search field; **New Project** creates one immediately. The project detail page lists all chats in the project and lets you start new chats directly within it. Chats started from the sidebar or the project overview are automatically assigned to the project. The context menu (⋯) per chat lets you move chats to another project or remove them from the project.

- **New Claude models**: Claude Opus 4.8 and Claude Opus 4.7 are now available in the model selection.

- **13 new Marketing & Sales Skills**: The skill library has been expanded with: Content Production, Marketing Demand & Acquisition, Marketing Strategy & PMM, Social Media Analyzer, Campaign Analytics, Video Content Strategist, Prompt Engineer Toolkit, Sales Engineer, Contract & Proposal Writer, Commercial Skills (Orchestrator), Pricing Strategist, Channel Economics, and Commercial Policy.

## 📄 companyFILES

- **Charts in company colors**: Newly created charts automatically apply the company colors defined in companyFILES Settings (Primary, Secondary, Accent). Bars, lines, and areas adapt to the configured color scheme.
