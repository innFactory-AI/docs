companyLEARN runs in CompanyGPT's shared interface. The sidebar with **New chat** and **All apps**, the account menu with theme and language, and the layout on small screens are the same for all addons and are described on [Shared interface](/en/company-gpt/addons/gemeinsame-oberflaeche/). This page only covers what companyLEARN itself adds.

## Menu

companyLEARN's menu has two entries for everyone:

| Entry | Content |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Start page with your courses, your progress and the next step |
| **Courses** | Course catalog with all published courses |

People with the **Manage courses** permission see a second group below, separated by a line:

| Entry | Content |
|---|---|
| **Course management** | All courses including drafts; create, edit, publish and delete courses |
| **Videos** | The video library: upload and delete videos |
| **API keys** | Create and revoke keys for the external REST API |

Without that permission, the second group is not shown. The available permissions are listed under [Permissions](/en/company-gpt/addons/companylearn/#permissions).

## Page header

Each page shows its title at the top. To the right are the page's actions, for example **New course** in course management, **Upload video** in the video library and **New key** on the API keys page.

## Language

The interface is available in German and English. On first use, companyLEARN takes over the chat's language, or the browser's if that is not available. companyLEARN remembers a change made in the account menu for future visits.

Course content itself – titles, texts, quiz questions – appears exactly as it was created; it is not translated.

## Embedded view in the chat

When the assistant opens a lesson, a quiz or a companyLEARN page in the chat, it appears right in the conversation, without sidebar and menu. Embedded lessons and quizzes show the title plus course and chapter at the top; at the end, below the content, the **Open full course** link opens the course in companyLEARN in a new tab. See [In the chat](/en/company-gpt/addons/companylearn/im-chat/) for details.
